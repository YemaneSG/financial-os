## Independent summary

PM-0 is a deliberately non-production proof of the two assumptions most capable
of invalidating the chosen stack: Firebase-issued exact-owner authorization in
Supabase and Plaid Hosted Link browser/native return. Receipt-system isolation is
explicit. Live bank data, synchronization, receipt integration, matching,
reflection, AI, production deployment, and public distribution are excluded.

## Strengths

- PM-0 retires architecture risk before product screens are expanded.
- Negative authorization, token lifecycle, grant, callback, and bundle evidence
  are specified before implementation.
- PM-0A can proceed on the current host while PM-0B prepares current native tools.
- The behavioral product remains awareness/reflection first with representative
  sampling and no predictive coaching.
- GitHub Project plus repository snapshots makes delivery reconstructable without
  an AI conversation.

## Remaining conditions

1. Owner approves architecture, security addendum, PM-0A packet, and external
   Sandbox actions before external changes.
2. PM-0 cannot be marked complete until PM-0B passes on current iOS tooling, a
   real iPhone, and the approved Android target.
3. Before PM-4, freeze measurable reflection burden, representative coverage,
   correction, and owner-usefulness outcomes.

## Explicit non-findings

- PM-0 does not require modifying the receipt collector.
- The absence of direct owner-facing value in PM-0A is acceptable because it is a
  bounded risk proof immediately preceding the first installable activity slice.
- No gateway, microservice split, Redis, event bus, LLM, or guidance schema is
  justified.

**Verdict: Approve with conditions**
